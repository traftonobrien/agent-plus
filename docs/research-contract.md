# Research Contract

A research contract is a pre-result agreement about what question the work can answer. It prevents a useful-looking analysis from changing target, population, split, or claim after results appear.

## Minimum fields

- Decision and research question.
- Null or abstention path.
- Population, source scope, and inclusion rules.
- Time boundary and leakage controls.
- Target, comparator, metric, and uncertainty treatment.
- Required evidence and prohibited claims.
- Stop conditions and promotion owner.

Use [the template](../templates/research-contract-template.md). A contract is incomplete when it cannot distinguish a positive, null, and blocked outcome.

## Change rule

If a material field changes, create a new contract version and explain why. Do not rewrite an old contract after viewing results.
