# Example: Honest BLOCK Outcome

> **Synthetic example.** A BLOCK documents incomplete evidence. It is not a negative baseball finding.

## Outcome

`BLOCK-COVERAGE-EXAMPLE`

## Why work stopped

The research contract requires complete date coverage before feature construction or evaluation. One required partition is missing and no independent inventory can explain the difference.

## What can be said

Coverage is unverified. No statement about model fit, future prediction, decision value, or real-world impact is permitted.

## What would unblock the work

A reviewable independent inventory must reconcile every required date partition and any documented exclusion. A fresh verifier then checks the revised evidence record.
