# Scouting Profile

## Required gates

1. Source provenance and observation scope.
2. Player evidence record with dates and limits.
3. Claim and uncertainty review.
4. Independent report check.
5. Handoff and update schedule.

## Required records

- Source log.
- Player evidence record.
- Claim-evidence register.
- Report review.
- Session handoff.

## Claim rule

Separate observation, projection, recommendation, and outcome. Do not promote a scouting narrative to a predictive claim without supporting evaluation.
