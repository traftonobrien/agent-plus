# Research Profile

## Required gates

1. Source provenance and population coverage.
2. Frozen target, temporal order, comparator, and metric contract.
3. Deterministic evaluation.
4. Fresh independent verification.
5. Claim-evidence register review.

## Required records

- Research contract.
- Source log.
- Evidence record.
- Independent review.
- Session handoff.

## Claim rule

Do not claim future prediction without chronological evaluation. Do not claim decision value or real-world impact without dedicated evidence.
