# Agent+ Output Policy

This policy controls public operating output.

1. Lead with the outcome or current action.
2. Use short numbered steps for multi-step work.
3. State verified evidence, limits, and one concrete next action.
4. Name `BLOCK` facts directly. Do not hide uncertainty with optimistic prose.
5. Preserve exact artifact IDs when they exist. Use only synthetic identifiers in this public package.
6. Do not expose secrets, raw data, private paths, private artifacts, or personal transcripts.
7. Do not claim model fit, future prediction, decision value, or real-world impact beyond recorded evidence.

The required closeout format is: outcome, evidence, limits, next action, and restart command.
