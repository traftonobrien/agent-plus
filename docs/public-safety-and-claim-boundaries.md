# Public Safety and Claim Boundaries

This package is public documentation. Do not add secrets, private datasets, personal transcripts, account identifiers, local absolute paths, proprietary artifacts, or unlicensed material.

## Examples are not results

Every sample in this repository is representative and synthetic. It illustrates a procedure. It does not validate a model or a baseball conclusion.

## Evidence ladder

| Level | Permitted question | Does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Model fit | Did a model fit its declared target on permitted data? | Future prediction or usefulness. |
| Future prediction | Did it improve chronological out-of-sample prediction against declared comparators? | Better decisions or operational impact. |
| Decision value | Did a defined decision policy improve under supported, measured conditions? | Real-world deployment impact. |
| Real-world impact | Did prospective use change an operational outcome? | Causality without an appropriate design. |

Never move a claim up this ladder because a lower-level metric is strong.

## BLOCK is an outcome

A `BLOCK` means required evidence, authorization, coverage, or verification is missing. It is not a failed attempt to hide or a result to repair into a positive finding. It protects readers from an unsupported claim and identifies the exact evidence needed to resume.

## Publication checklist

Before publishing, check that the package has no sensitive material, all examples are labeled, claims match evidence, and external tooling is credited as external.
